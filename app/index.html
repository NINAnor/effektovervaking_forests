This project's near-real-time monitor. Each dot is a 10 m pixel where the Sentinel-2 signal broke from its seasonal pattern: loss when tree cover fell (felling, windthrow, dieback), gain when it grew back.

Only breaks larger than the magnitude gate are shown, minus any a reviewer rejected. A dot is dated by its latest break. Confidence (0–1) grows as later images confirm the break.
